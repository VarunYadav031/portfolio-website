Deepfake Sign-Language Detection

(Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, TensorFlow, CNN + LSTM)

Key outcome: A detector that flags manipulated sign-language videos with high recall.

  • Preprocessing: extracted frame-wise hand & pose landmarks using MediaPipe and normalized keypoints.
  • Feature engineering: temporal sequences of landmarks + velocity/acceleration of joints as model inputs.
  • Model: CNN to encode per-frame spatial features + LSTM to learn temporal patterns; trained with cross-entropy loss.
  • Robustness: applied data augmentation (temporal warping, noise, occlusion) and adversarial-sample checks.
  • Evaluation: measured precision/recall and used confusion matrices to tune thresholds for deployment.

Possible artifacts detected: mismatch in motion dynamics, inconsistent finger articulation, and temporal jitter.
